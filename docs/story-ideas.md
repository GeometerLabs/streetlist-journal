# Story ideas

A backlog for the owner and the Journal's writers to pick from. **Not published on the site, but this repository is public**, so
anything written here can be read by anyone: keep it to ideas, never people's details. Each idea follows
[editorial.md](editorial.md): civic, never partisan; information, never advice where the subject is legal, medical or financial; no
criticism of a named institution; respect for each community's customs. Started with the owner's ideas of 14 Nov 2026.

| Section | Working title | The idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community and civic life | **Getting to your hearing on time** | Helping people with a case in a local court keep up with their hearings: the day's cause list read out in their own language, a reminder the evening before, the room and the time. Information only, never legal advice; the advocate stays the person who advises. |
| Community and civic life | **The first six hours** | Funerals and last rites: the calls, the arrangements and the people to tell in the first hours, coordinated so the family can grieve. Written with care for each community's customs, which the family and their elders decide. |
| Livelihoods | **A wedding's thousand messages** | Weddings and community events: the caterer, the decorator, the tent house and the band, their timings and their deposits, kept in one place. Who confirms what, and what happens when a date moves. |
| Livelihoods | **Learning for the job down the road** | A course pack for adults at a local centre: practice between classes for the jobs nearby, paid once, with the teacher in charge. Better opportunities close to home, without leaving town to find them. Builds on the training centre piece. |
| Community and civic life | **One tractor, many fields** | Equipment hire for farmers: a shared machine booked fairly in a short season, with deposits, repairs and a record members can question. A follow-up to the tractor bookings piece, once a group tries it on paper. |
| Technology | **Prices before sunrise** | A morning mandi price line for a farmers' WhatsApp group: the day's prices by grade with the time they were noted, the weather, and crop questions passed to a person. Run by a local partner, at a price the group agrees. |
