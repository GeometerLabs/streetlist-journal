# Pictures for the Journal: art direction and prompts

For the owner, who generates or commissions each piece's lead art and each section's header. Until a piece has its art, it keeps
what it has now: `salon-whatsapp-deposits` keeps its photograph (Lekhak Anurag / Unsplash), and the other five show a cover drawn
from their companion. Nothing here is generated automatically.

## The spirit

Busy, affectionate, gently funny scenes of ordinary people in Indian towns and villages, where technology is simply part of the day:
a phone propped on a tea glass, a price board beside a sack of onions, a tractor's owner checking a booking while a child
climbs the seat. Many small figures, each doing something; nobody is the butt of the joke. Dignity, warmth, crowds, weather, animals,
signboards with no readable words. Technology is never the hero; people are.

We describe the qualities we want and never copy or imitate a named artist's work. No prompt names an artist.

## Paste before every prompt

> Hand-drawn editorial illustration for a warm, humorous magazine about life in Indian towns and villages. Confident pen-and-ink
> line work with loose watercolour washes, warm natural colours on off-white paper, gentle humour, many small ordinary people each
> busy with something, everyday technology (phones, a laptop, a QR standee, a price board) quietly part of the scene. Crowded but
> clear, one obvious centre of attention. Landscape.

**Negative prompt:** text, words, letters, numbers, logos, brands, watermark, readable screens, user interface, photorealism, 3D
render, CGI, glossy digital painting, neon, glow, gradient sky, robots, holograms, caricature of poverty, caste or religious symbols
as decoration, mockery, celebrities, real people's likeness, political symbols, flags, extra fingers.

## Sizes, files and how to add them

| What | Shape | Ideal size (smallest) | File name in `content/media/` | Where in Pages CMS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A piece's lead art | 3:2 | 2400 x 1600 (1600 x 1067) | `<piece address>.jpg`, e.g. `village-tractor-bookings.jpg` | Pieces > English > the piece > Lead picture |
| A section's header | 3:1 | 2400 x 800 (1800 x 600) | `section-<id>.jpg`, e.g. `section-livelihoods.jpg` | Section pictures > the section > Header picture |

JPEG (quality 85 or more) or PNG. Keep the important part inside the middle 70%: the site crops to the frame and shows the lead
large on a laptop and small on cards. In Pages CMS, upload the file, then fill **Alt text** (what the picture shows, one plain
sentence) and **Credit** ("Drawing: Name", or "Image generated with <tool> for the Streetlist Journal"), with **Credit link** if the
maker has a page. Saving publishes it within a few minutes.

## Lead art, piece by piece

**`training-centre-interview-packs`** (Livelihoods). A training centre up a narrow staircase above a market: a teacher at a
whiteboard with a drawn hotel reception desk, learners in pairs rehearsing a job interview, one nervous young man in a borrowed tie
practising into his phone in the corridor while a friend pretends to be the manager, a ceiling fan turning, chappals in a heap at
the door, the market noise below.
**Alt:** "A busy training centre above a market, where learners rehearse job interviews in pairs and one practises on his phone."

**`town-owned-ai-service`** (Community and civic life). A members' meeting under a neem tree beside a shared tractor shed: farmers on
a durrie and plastic chairs, one reading from a fat register, another raising a hand to object, a woman keeping the accounts in a
ledger, a phone being passed along the row, tea arriving on a tray, a dog asleep under the tractor.
**Alt:** "Members of an equipment group meet under a tree beside their shared tractor, passing a phone and a register along the row."

**`paid-work-around-an-agent`** (Livelihoods). A young local operator's corner desk in a wholesaler's office: a laptop between two
boxes of different sizes, a wall clock, a shopkeeper arriving with a crumpled delivery slip, a phone ringing, a delivery boy waiting
with a handcart outside the door, a calendar with many circled days.
**Alt:** "A local operator at a cluttered desk sorts out a delivery slip with a shopkeeper while the phone rings."

**`market-orders-by-voice`** (Livelihoods). Early morning in a wholesale market lane: a shopkeeper holding a phone close to his mouth
to record a voice note, stacked oil tins and sacks, a handcart boy waiting, a clerk with a carbon-copy book at the wholesaler's
counter, two boxes of the same oil in different sizes, a cow ambling past.
**Alt:** "A shopkeeper records a voice note in a busy wholesale lane, with stacked oil tins and a clerk writing at the counter."

**`salon-whatsapp-deposits`** (Livelihoods; keeps its photograph until the art arrives). A small-town salon on a wedding-season
evening: an aunt bargaining at the counter, a stylist pinning flowers into a bride's hair, two cousins taking photos in the mirror,
a phone on the counter lit with a message bubble, a wall calendar crowded with marked dates, steel tumblers of tea.
**Alt:** "A crowded salon in wedding season: a stylist pins flowers into a bride's hair while an aunt bargains at the counter."

**`village-tractor-bookings`** (Community and civic life). A village edge at harvest: one tractor and five farmers, each pointing to
their own field, the owner checking his phone with a pencil behind his ear, a child on the tractor seat, goats in the way, a flask
of tea on the mudguard, rain clouds gathering over the hills.
**Alt:** "Five farmers point to their own fields around one tractor while its owner checks the bookings on his phone."

## Section headers

**Technology** (`section-technology.jpg`). A street of small repair shops at dusk: phones and radios open on the counters, a
soldering iron's thin smoke, customers waiting on a bench, a boy on a bicycle delivering a part, string lights coming on.

**Livelihoods** (`section-livelihoods.jpg`). A mandi at dawn: vegetables in heaps, weighing scales, a price board with no readable
words, traders on phones, a tea seller weaving through, a tempo backing in with someone guiding it.

**Community and civic life** (`section-community.jpg`). A ward meeting on a school veranda in the evening: people on mats and benches,
someone holding up a hand-drawn map, a child asleep on a lap, a tea kettle, the notice board behind them with no readable words.

**Ideas and philosophy** (`section-ideas.jpg`). A bus stop on a hot afternoon: people of every age waiting and talking at once, one
reading a book, two arguing happily over a newspaper with no readable words, a vendor selling roasted peanuts, the bus just
appearing at the bend.

**Art and culture** (`section-art.jpg`). A lane on a festival evening: a signboard painter at work, a potter's wheel, a group
rehearsing a song with a harmonium, children making paper lanterns, a phone filming it all from a balcony.
